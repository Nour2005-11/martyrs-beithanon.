# About Beit Hanoun: source notes

The public About page uses cautious, original wording and links readers directly to the following sources. It does not copy their prose or use their images as local documentary assets.

The [Beit Hanoun Municipality city page](https://beithanoun.ps/Beithanoun-city) identifies the city as being in the far north-east of the Gaza Strip, describes its agricultural identity, and provides municipal context for the city’s relationship to citrus, olives, vineyards, and fertile land. The page also situates the city relative to Beit Lahia and Jabalia.

[Palestine Open Maps](https://palopenmaps.org/ar/maps/bayt-hannun) provides a historic-map exploration entry for Beit Hanoun in the Gaza district. The [Palestine Remembered entry](https://www.palestineremembered.com/GeoPoints/Bayt_Hanun_861/ar/index.html) links historical maps, photographs, and source references, including British Mandate village statistics. The [Palestine Archive map record](https://www.palarchive.org/index.php/Detail/objects/91179/lang/en_US) describes a Map of Beith Hanoun and its mapped topography, roads, and planting types.

[Wikimedia Commons’ Beit Hanun category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Beit_Hanun) is linked as a source catalogue only. Any future reuse of a specific image must be assessed on that image’s own file page for author, date, and license. No Commons image is copied into this project in Stage 2.
